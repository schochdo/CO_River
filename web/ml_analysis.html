How Many Days Until Water Reaches Lake Powell?

Arrival Delay: 1.5 to 8 Days

Water released from upstream headwaters takes up to 8 days to travel through canyon corridors and fill Lake Powell.

100 Years of Wet Water vs. Paper Promises (1922–2026)

Met 26% of 100 Yrs • 3.7% Since 2000

The 1922 Compact promised 16.5 million acre-feet every year, but actual river flow met that target only 26% of the time—and only once since 2000.

Why a 2-Day Flash Flood Doesn't Fill Lake Powell

Snowmelt: 1.35 MAF vs. Flash: 0.02 MAF

A sharp 2-day desert flash flood delivers only 0.02 MAF (drained in 30 hours by mandatory releases), while sustained 60-day snowmelt fills 1.35 MAF.
