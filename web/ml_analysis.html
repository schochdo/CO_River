4. Area Under the Curve: 48-Hr Flash Flood vs. 60-Day Snowmelt

V = ∫ Q dt

Why a 5,000 cfs desert rainstorm spike adds negligible volume compared to sustained spring snowmelt.

Spring Snowmelt Pulse 1.35 MAF
Sustained 45–60 day snowpack crest (15,000–21,000 cfs) yields 1,350,000 AF.
48-Hr Desert Flash Flood 0.02 MAF
5,000 cfs spike adds only 20,000 AF — drained by Lees Ferry release in 30 hours!
Physical Reality: Volume equals the time-integral of discharge ($V = \int Q \, dt$). The spring snowmelt delivers 67× more water than a fierce desert cloudburst. Only winter snowpack can replenish Lake Powell.
