Hydrologic & Legal Context

The 1922 Compact allocated 15.0 to 16.5 million acre-feet of "paper water" during an anomalously wet decade. In reality, the 21st-century average runoff is only ~12.3 million acre-feet, creating the persistent basin-wide structural deficit documented in Cadillac Desert.
