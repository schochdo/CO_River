## Methodological Summary
* **Human-in-the-Loop Control:** Architecture, research questions, and design decisions (such as focusing on trans-mountain diversions, 19th-century mining law seniority, and the 10-minute presentation deck structure) were directed and prioritized by the human author.
* **Code Verification & Automated Testing:** Every feature and refactor was verified via headless Chrome DevTools Protocol (CDP) scripts, visual regression image diffs, automated mass-balance sanity checks, and local Python validation suites.
* **Domain & Dimensional Rigor:** Maintained strict dimensional separation between instantaneous discharge rates ($Q$ in cfs) and annualized cumulative volumes ($V$ in MAF/yr). Adhered to physical hydrologic mass-balance laws at all confluences.
* **Environment Integrity:** Code was executed strictly within an isolated local virtual environment (`.venv`) and verified on local HTTP servers without relying on third-party SaaS hosting.
