## 4. Methodological Summary
* **Human-in-the-Loop Control:** Research questions, legal/hydrologic scope boundaries (such as separating mainstream Compact accounting from off-mainstem Gila Basin depletions under *Arizona v. California* and standardizing contemporary accounting onto the 25-year `2000–2024` Millennium Drought baseline), and editorial layout choices were directed and audited by the human author; AI models served as implementation and verification pair-programmers.
* **Multi-Model Division of Labor:** Fast models (`Gemini 3.8 Flash`, `Gemini 4 Neon Low`) handled high-frequency UI/CSS iterations and data scripting; reasoning and review models (`Gemini Web Argon`, `Claude Opus 5.5`, `Gemini 4 Carbon`) audited hydrologic mass-balance closure, statutory citations, and cross-file regressions.
* **Automated Verification Gate (`scripts/release_check.sh`):** Every release verifies SHA-256 raw-file checksums (`data/raw/MANIFEST.csv`), SQLite-to-JSON numerical parity across all 10 static contracts, executed notebook PNG outputs (`>= 4` figures), and headless Chrome DevTools Protocol (`CDP`) rendering across 9 pages × 2 viewports (`0` uncaught errors, `0` horizontal overflow, `0` banned marketing superlatives).
* **Dimensional Rigor:** Instantaneous discharge rates (`Q` in `cfs`) and cumulative/annualized volumes (`V` in `AF` or `MAF/yr`) are strictly separated via `1 cfs sustained for 1 year = 723.97 AF ≈ 0.000724 MAF`.
